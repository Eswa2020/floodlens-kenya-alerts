# G07 Duty officer approval protocol
Every alert drafted by FloodLens starts with approved_by set to null and status pending_approval. Only an authenticated officer can approve. Before approving, the officer checks the evidence score, the county, the severity and whether the report was routed for language or injection review. Kiswahili and Sheng reports are always read by a person. Reports with low agreement are not deleted; they stay in the low priority queue and are re-checked when new reports arrive from the same area. A missed flood costs more than a false alarm, so when in doubt the officer escalates to the county disaster office.
